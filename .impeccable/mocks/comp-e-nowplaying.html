Tonality by ear:
pentatonic, major,
minor, Aeolian

By the end of this lesson you can

Hear major versus minor reliably, identify pentatonic and Aeolian, and say what you heard that told you.

01

Play C major, then C minor on keyboard. Name the note that changed (the third).

02

Play the black keys only to hear a pentatonic scale.

03

Play A to A on white keys to hear Aeolian.

04

Sing each scale up and down, then identify a teacher-played example by ear.
