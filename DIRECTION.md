# Direction

**Retired 19 October 2026.** This site is on the shared music shell, direction B, Stage Card. The current direction and its rules live in `../music-shell/README.md` and `../.serve/proto/redesign/SHELL-PLAN.md`; what is still this site's own is in `DESIGN.md`.

This file used to hold the contract for **On Air**, the world the site was built in from 11 August 2026, which `build.py` emitted as an HTML comment at the top of every page. The build no longer emits it. The contract as written is in this file's history (any commit up to `d2bb9eb`).

## What carried over

Two of On Air's rules are content rules rather than look, and still hold:

1. **Twenty works on the site, never twenty-two.** The register's examination column and its two held-back works are internal. Putting them on a public site leaks AT3.
2. **Position is never carried by colour alone.** The shell holds this for every site: the current lesson says "Up to here" in words, and every section carries an icon and a word.
