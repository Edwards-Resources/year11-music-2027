Lesson 10: Aural and theory drills: metre, tempo and rhythm

Lesson 10 of 50 · Theoretical language

Learning intention

Hear and notate simple and compound metre, identify tempo and changes in tempo, and recognise and write down common rhythmic patterns.

By the end you can: Tell simple from compound metre, name the tempo, and notate a short rhythm you hear.

Outcomes MU1-11L-02

Success criteria

Tick them off as you go.

In classThis fortnight

  1. Listen and decide: simple or compound metre?
  2. Tap the beat and name the tempo.
  3. Clap back a two-bar pattern, then notate it.
  4. Self-check against the answer your teacher shows.

Listening

Teacher’s choice

Aural skill grows with short, regular practice. Most lessons open with this 10-minute drill; this page is the reference for the rhythm and metre content running through the term.
