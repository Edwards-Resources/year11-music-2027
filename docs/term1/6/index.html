Success criteria

From exploring to interpreting

In the first half of the term you explored how context shapes music. Now you turn to interpretation: how performers and audiences make meaning from a work, and how that meaning shifts with context.

ICIP protocol

Where any activity uses Aboriginal or Torres Strait Islander music or cultural material, follow NESA ICIP protocols. Work in partnership with Elders, Community members, Cultural Knowledge Holders or your local AECG, and do not present sacred or restricted material.
