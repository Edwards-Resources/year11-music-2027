Success criteria

Expressive devices toolkit

DeviceMeaningTry it
DynamicsHow loud or soft (p, f, crescendo, diminuendo).Grow louder into a phrase peak, then ease back.
ArticulationHow notes are attacked and joined (staccato, legato, accent).Play a phrase staccato, then legato; hear the change.
PhrasingShaping notes into musical sentences.Breathe or lift at the end of each phrase.
Vibrato / muteColour added to a sustained note or timbre.Add a little vibrato on long notes where the style allows.
