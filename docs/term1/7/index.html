Lesson 7: Performance: technique and expression for style

Lesson 7 of 50 · Performing

Learning intention

Perform using techniques appropriate to the style and genre, shape a performance with dynamics, articulation and phrasing, and adjust your playing in response to feedback.

By the end you can: Perform a short piece with technique that suits the style, make one clear expressive choice, and act on feedback.

Outcomes MU1-11P-01 MU1-11P-02

Success criteria

Tick them off as you go.

In classThis fortnight

  1. Week 2: technique for style, clean and controlled playing that fits the idiom.
  2. Week 4: expression through dynamics, articulation and phrasing.
  3. Logbook entry each workshop: what you performed, one technique or expressive choice you worked on, feedback received, one goal for next time.
  4. Keep your logbook current; it is your evidence of progress toward AT1.

Listening

Teacher’s choice

A performing lesson: you are the listening example. These workshops also rehearse your AT1 performance checkpoint.
