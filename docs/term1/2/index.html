Success criteria

Compositional language by family

FamilyDevices you should know nowWhat it does
Rhythmicostinato, syncopation, swing, backbeat, groove, tripletsDrives the music and gives it feel.
Melodicriff, hook, sequence, imitation, melismaShapes the tune and makes it memorable.
Texturalunison, layering, countermelody, doubling, loopingControls how many layers you hear and how they combine.
Structuralmotif, theme, verse–chorus, call and response, binary, ternaryOrganises the music over time.

Listen for these four first

DeviceQuick definitionListen for
OstinatoA short pattern repeated through a passage.A riff or rhythm that keeps coming back unchanged.
SyncopationAccents placed off the main beat.The push or surprise when a note lands between beats.
RiffA short, catchy repeated melodic or rhythmic idea.A guitar or keyboard hook that anchors the song.
SequenceA melodic idea repeated higher or lower.The same shape moving up or down in steps.
