Success criteria

Ways to develop a motif

ApproachWhat to try
RhythmicAdd syncopation, turn it into an ostinato, or augment the note values.
MelodicMove it up or down as a sequence, invert it, or turn it into a riff.
TexturalAdd a countermelody, double it an octave up, or loop it.
HarmonicPlace it over I, IV, V or vi and hear how the mood changes.

Documenting is assessable practice

For each sketch, save a new version and write one or two lines: what the idea is, what device you used, and what you will try next. This habit feeds directly into the Year 11 composition development requirements.

ICIP protocol

Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander musical material must only be used following ICIP protocols and consultation. Do not imitate or reproduce restricted material in your sketches.
