Term 1 · Week 4 · Lesson 4 of 50 Contexts of music
Harmony by ear: chordsI, IV, V and vi
ListeningNot on the register

No work from the register is pinned to this lesson; a teacher-chosen song carries the identification activity in class.

What you will be able to do

Find the tonic, hear a move to IV, V or vi, and play a I–V–vi–IV progression.

OutcomesMU1-11L-02
In class this fortnight
01

Find the tonic (I). Hum the note that feels like home.

02

When the chord changes, ask: does it feel like it is moving away (IV), pulling back home (V), or going darker (vi)?

03

Play the bass note: the lowest note often names the chord.

04

Test your guess by playing the progression on a keyboard, then identify it in a teacher-chosen song by ear.
