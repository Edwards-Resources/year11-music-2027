Lesson 47: Examination feedback and error analysis

Lesson 47 of 50 · Listening, analysing and responding

Learning intention

Analyse errors from AT3 Part A by question and by element, and identify the two features most often missed.

By the end you can: Name the two features of aural analysis you most often miss, sorted from your own AT3 Part A error analysis.

Outcomes MU1-11L-02

Success criteria

Tick them off as you go.

In classThis fortnight

  1. Return AT3 Part A with rubric feedback through Canvas.
  2. Sort personal errors by question and by musical element (rhythm, harmony, melody, texture, structure, expression).
  3. Identify the two features most often missed, individually.
  4. Class re-teaches the item most students missed, drawn from the whole cohort’s error pattern.

Listening

Teacher’s choice

Confirmed in class.

LearnWhy this matters

An error analysis is worth more than a mark. Returning a mark alone tells a student how they did; sorting errors by question and by musical element tells them specifically what to fix, which is what actually improves the next attempt.
