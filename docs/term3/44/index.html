Lesson 44: Individual study: research and analysis

Lesson 44 of 50 · Listening, analysing and responding

Learning intention

Research the chosen screen composer, sub-genre or tradition, and analyse its repertoire against the taught core, justifying observations with musical evidence.

By the end you can: State an observation about your individual study topic and back it with a specific, named musical feature from the repertoire.

Outcomes MU1-11L-01 MU1-11L-02

Success criteria

Tick them off as you go.

In classThis fortnight

  1. Research the composer, sub-genre or tradition confirmed in Week 2, using reliable sources.
  2. Identify specific musical features of its repertoire and compare them against the term’s taught core (leitmotif, notation, scoring technology, cultural tradition).
  3. Draft interpretative observations, each backed by a named musical feature, ready for the teacher conference.
  4. Teacher conference: check that each observation is defensible with evidence, not only asserted.

Listening

Teacher’s choice

Confirmed in class.

LearnHow the session runs

Independent research is defended the same way class analysis is: an observation is only useful if it can be backed with a specific musical feature, named and located in the repertoire. This is a working session with a teacher conference for each student, not a free research period.
