Lesson 43: Performance workshop: refining from recordings

Lesson 43 of 50 · Performing

Learning intention

Record the AT3 piece and analyse it against a professional recording, and expand the material where the piece calls for it.

By the end you can: Compare a recording of your AT3 piece against a professional recording, and make at least one specific adjustment as a result.

Outcomes MU1-11P-01 MU1-11P-02

Success criteria

Tick them off as you go.

In classThis fortnight

  1. Record a full run-through of the AT3 piece.
  2. Compare the recording against a professional recording of the same or a stylistically similar work, identifying specific differences.
  3. Adjust the performance based on what the comparison reveals.
  4. Where the piece calls for it, expand the material (an added introduction, an improvised link, or a repeat treated differently) and log the decision.

Listening

Teacher’s choice

Confirmed in class.

LearnWhy this matters

Hearing yourself back is the fastest form of feedback available: a recording exposes gaps between what a performer intends and what actually happens that are hard to notice while playing.
