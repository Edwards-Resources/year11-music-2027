Why this matters

Accurate vocabulary is what makes analysis fast under examination conditions: naming a feature precisely, on the first attempt, saves time that a vague description costs. The first Year 12 listen, unmarked, previews the focus area taught first from Term 4.

In class this fortnight

  • 01Short quiz on this unit’s vocabulary (diegetic and non-diegetic, cue, spotting, sync point, leitmotif, notation systems) and the year’s Music language.
  • 02First listen to a work from Music of the last 25 years (Australian focus), the Year 12 focus area taught first from Term 4, without formal analysis.
  • 03Class discussion: what do students already notice, using this year’s vocabulary, without being asked to?
