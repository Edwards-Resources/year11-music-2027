Lesson 17: DAWs, virtual instruments and effects as sound sources

Lesson 17 of 50 · Creating and composing

Learning intention

Realise the same short idea in three different environments, and compare timbre and texture across an acoustic, a notated and a produced version of it.

By the end you can: Realise the same idea in three environments, and explain in the log one difference in timbre or texture each environment produced.

Outcomes MU1-11C-01 MU1-11C-02

Success criteria

Tick them off as you go.

In classThis fortnight

  1. Compose or select a four-bar idea from Week 1 or Week 2 material.
  2. Realise it acoustically (sung or played live), notated in flat.io, and produced in a DAW using virtual instruments and effects.
  3. Compare the three versions for timbre and texture: what changed, and what the environment made easy or hard to do.
  4. Log entry recording the comparison and which environment best served the student’s musical intent for this idea.

Listening

Teacher’s choice

Confirmed in class.

LearnWhy this matters

The environment a piece is made in is a creative decision, not a neutral container. The same four bars sound and behave differently played live, notated and played back through flat.io, or built in a DAW with virtual instruments and effects, because each environment makes some choices easy and others hard.
