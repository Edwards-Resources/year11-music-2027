Riff versus hook

TermWhat it isJob it does
RiffA short repeated instrumental or rhythmic figure.Drives the groove, often underneath other material.
HookThe single most memorable moment in a song, vocal or instrumental.Is what a listener remembers and hums back.

Why this matters

Songwriting is a compositional process with its own conventions, distinct from writing an idea for its own sake. A chorus is not just louder; it is built to lift, usually through a rise in register, a simpler or more open harmonic rhythm, and a hook placed where it lands hardest.

In class this fortnight

  • 01Select a hook generated during Week 2’s improvisation session.
  • 02Set a verse lyric or vocalised idea to rhythm, deliberately choosing where stressed syllables fall against strong beats.
  • 03Build a chorus from the hook that lifts against the verse: register, harmonic rhythm, or dynamic.
  • 04Draft in flat.io or a DAW and log which songwriting decisions were made and why.
